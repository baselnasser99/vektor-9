# Vektor-9 audit plan

Read-only audit, 2026-11-11. Nothing in the repo or the open Blender file was changed. Measurements come from the live Blender scene (via MCP), the shipped glTF files in `web/assets/models/`, the renderer's own layout code (`cityLayout`, `STREETS`) run in the browser on seed 7 at tick 4032, and `?bench`.

## 0. Scope notes

- **AWS is not built yet.** `infra/` and `lambdas/` do not exist. The 5-minute tick runs only locally (`uv run python -m sim`, `TICKS_PER_DAY = 288` in `sim/rules.py:6`). The plan (`PROJECT_PLAN.md:84-111`, milestone M1) is:
  - EventBridge Scheduler every 5 minutes triggers a tick Lambda (reserved concurrency 1).
  - The Lambda writes `latest.json` and immutable snapshots to a private S3 bucket behind CloudFront (OAC).
  - Marks come in through API Gateway, a mark Lambda, DynamoDB and SQS (M4).
  - A CloudWatch alarm fires if no tick arrives for 15 minutes.
- **The open Blender file is not the city.** It is an unsaved file holding the interchange set pieces:
  - Five scenes: `ix_depot`, `ix_gantry`, `ix_monument`, `ix_stock` and `ix_wall`.
  - A default `Scene` with a 2 m `Cube` and material `Material`.
  - 64 objects and 18 materials in total.

  The city's streets and blocks exist only in the browser, built from the snapshot, so all road, block and crossing measurements below come from the renderer's layout code, not from Blender.
- **Source of truth for models.** Every model is generated by a script in `tools/blender/` using `kit.py`, then exported as `.gltf` + `.bin`.
  - Fixes go into the scripts, followed by a re-export, never by hand in a `.blend`.
  - The ix pieces come from `tools/blender/interchange_props.py`.
- **Skills used:**
  - Blender: `blender-director` (production brief and QA workflow), with polycount budgets from its references.
  - Urban: `street-design`, `block-and-density` and `design-evaluation`.
  - `sci-fi-punk-worlds` was not loaded. The aesthetic judgement below follows `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md` instead.
  - These skill packs are installed and available. Further ones were not loaded: `blender-asset-qa`, `asset-optimization`, `lod-pipeline`, `hard-surface`, `environment-artist`, `urban-design-foundations`, `mobility-and-transport`, `public-space-design` and the rest of the packs.

## 1. Architecture summary (what the fixes must respect)

- **Grid.**
  - 48 x 48 tiles (`SIZE = 48`), in 8 x 8 sectors (`BLOCK = 8`, 36 sectors). `SCHEMA = 4`.
  - Each tile holds `k, f, t, p, on, s, b`: kind, floors, target floors, residents, powered, style seed and the tick it took its current form (ADR-0003).
  - Kinds are `lot, build, house, market, plant`, plus the fixed terrain kinds `rail, park, works, road`.
  - Streets are not tiles; the renderer draws them.
- **Renderer layout.**
  - `TILE = 14` m, with building footprints of 7.5–12.5 m. A floor is 3.5 m.
  - Sector-line corridors are the ring, main street, walk/lane and boulevard (avenue near the centre). A mid street (local, one-way or alley) runs every 4 tiles.
  - On seed 7 the city spans 874 x 874 m (76.4 ha), with sector lines at ±429, ±292.5, ±150 and 0 m.
- **Model contract.**
  - `kit.model(name, roles)` swaps each mesh's material by its **material name**, which is the role, and throws if a role is missing.
  - Small parts sit under an empty named `detail`, which is folded or LOD'd.
  - Origin is at ground centre; metres, y-up; `export_apply=True`; no tangents.
  - All city meshes are merged per material (`mergeByMaterial`), so per-model draw calls mostly vanish and **triangles are the real cost**.

### Defects found

1. **ix_gantry: 32 floating truss posts (real bug).**
   - In `interchange_props.py` the verticals loop `for k in range(-15, 16)` places posts at x = 2k, out to ±30 m. The truss chords only span ±15.65 m (`2 * span + 0.5`, with `span = 15.4`).
   - The posts at |x| = 16–30 m (k = ±8..±15, both faces) hang at 5.85–6.85 m with nothing under them, at y = ±0.35.
   - That is 384 of the gantry's 1,052 triangles (37%), and it shows on both gantries over the expressway.
2. **ix_monument: hanging light short of its tie.** The light (`glow_cyan` block 0.16 x 0.6 x 0.16, centre 8.55 m) ends at 8.85 m, but the titanium tie's underside is at 8.93 m. That leaves an 8 cm gap at (0, 0).
3. **ix_depot: work lamps floating.** The two `lamp` blocks at x = ±3.0, height 4.6 (top 4.66), sit 0.165 m below the steel canopy (underside 4.825). They are also about 0.125 m off the wall: they are placed at `d/2 + 0.25` with 0.25 m depth.
4. **ix_monument: seam tops flush with blade tops (minor z-fighting).** The `glow_cyan` seam tubes end exactly on the graphite blade tops at 22.0 m and 16.5 m, at about x = ±0.75. The overlap is about 0.006 m² each, so a faint flicker is possible up close.
5. **Coplanar faces that are hidden, so not visible.**
   - ix_depot: the titanium and hazard undersides at 0.3 m rest on the slab top.
   - ix_wall: the concrete and graphite undersides sit at 0 m.

   No action is needed beyond the triangle cost.
6. **Open boundaries, which are expected.**
   - ix_monument: `concrete.002` and `graphite.003` each have 48 boundary edges, most likely open bottoms resting on the plinth or ground. This is not verified face by face.
   - ix_gantry: `panel` and `panel_b` are single-sided message screens.
7. **Mesh hygiene, which is harmless.** ix_monument `glow_cyan` has 12 duplicate verts (ring seams) and 8 n-gon caps. ix_stock has a few 4+-sided caps.
8. **File hygiene.** The `.blend` is unsaved, and the stray default `Scene` (Cube + `Material`) and the `Dots Stroke` grease-pencil material are left over. The repo's glTFs are the deliverable, so this file can be discarded after the fixes are re-exported.

### Defects and opportunities

1. **HQ hidden stacked caps (wasted triangles, not visible).** Each module keeps its top faces where the next module stacks on it. Same-facing coplanar overlaps, triangle-centre test:
   - `hq_base_*` at y = 28: hull/panel about 148 m², graphite/panel about 92 m², graphite/hull about 79 m².
   - `hq_hangar_*` at y = 24: graphite/hull about 324 m².
   - `hq_shaft_b_*` at y = 24: fin/hull about 43 m².

   Dropping caps that are always covered saves triangles without changing the look.
2. **HQ roof deck: possible z-fighting.**
   - `hq_roof_l` and `hq_roof_r` each have about 720 m² of `dark` and `panel` faces coplanar and same-facing at local y = 10.25, near (-4, 10.25, 5).
   - `hq_plaza` has about 201 m² of hull and stone coplanar at ground level (y = 0, near (-21.3, 0, 14.7)).
   - Neither flickered in browser stills (seed 7, HQ at world (115, -80.5), heading 90°), but a close orbit is needed to clear them.
3. **Docs drift.** The `web/src/headquarters.js` header says "4 x 3 tiles", but ADR-0007 specifies a 6 x 4, 24-tile campus. The handoff also notes the campus is 8 m wider than the model.
4. **Heavy CC0 pieces aren't in the city.** `modular_airduct_circular_01` (14,280 triangles), `modular_fire_escape` (11,322), `modular_industrial_pipes_01` (12,340) and `utility_box_01` (4,404) load only on `sample.html` and the showcase page. They need no action unless they come into the city.

## 4. Streets, crossings and blocks

The renderer's `STREETS` table (`web/src/world.js:10-20`) compared with NACTO / GDCI ranges. Lanes are 3.25 m everywhere, inside the 3.0–3.3 m urban range.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| boulevard | 36 | 6.5 sidewalk | 3 + 3 lanes, 9.75 each way | 3.5 | 5.0 | OK. Arterial ROW is 30–40 m and a refuge needs ≥ 1.8 m. |
| avenue | 36 | 6.5 | 2 + 2 lanes | 10 | 5.0 | OK (landscaped median 2.4–6 m+). |

- **Crossings:** zebras are 3 m wide (standard), with stop lines 1.2 m behind the zebra. Corner radii of 3.5–5 m are standard urban, and 5 m on the boulevard suits buses. These are fine.
- **Lamps:** poles are 6 m everywhere (`streetLamp`). That is pedestrian scale; roadway lighting would be 8–12 m on the boulevard and main streets. This is a style choice. Taller poles are optional and would add triangles.
- **Blocks:**
  - Each quarter-block has 56 x 56 m of land (4 tiles), about 63 x 63 m at the kerb, so the block perimeter is **about 250 m**. That is at the fine end of the 250–500 m walkable range, which is good for walkers.
  - Sector pitch is 136.5–150 m between sector lines.
  - Intersection density is about 13 corridors per axis over 0.76 km², roughly **220 crossings per km²**, against a target of over 100. This is excellent even after counting out walks and alleys.
- **Density and height (seed 7, tick 4032):**
  - 1,442 houses, median 12 floors (p75 16, p95 22, max 31), 569 empty lots.
  - Estimated FAR is about **2.8** against the gross 76.4 ha, which is urban.
- **Unrealistic by intent (flag, don't fix):**
  - Towers on 14 m lots with footprints up to 12.5 m leave gaps of 1.5–6.5 m between 40–100 m towers. Privacy guidance is 18–22 m.
  - Street height-to-width on main streets is about 2:1 (12-floor walls along a 21 m corridor), past Gehl's 1:1–1:3.

  Both are the intended cyberpunk canyon, not bugs. Keep them, but don't add more bulk to the narrow streets.
- **Known connection problems (from the handoff and this session):**
  1. Walkers pass through a ramp's low end where it crosses the main street's sidewalk (interchange).
  2. Ramp merges don't look for a gap, so cars bunch up briefly.
  3. Walkers route between sector corners only, not along the mid streets.
  4. Dirt-track cars don't tilt on slopes.

## 5. Frame time (ADR-0005, target 16.7 ms)

- `?bench` today: **1,293 draw calls and 9.2M triangles per frame**, including shadow passes. 52–75 ms per frame here, depending on pane size and GPU sharing.
  - ADR-0004 recorded about 5.8M triangles and 410 draw calls, so both have grown a lot since.
  - The biggest triangle materials are steel (2.4–2.6M), concrete (0.86M), glow (0.55M), MeshBasic (0.46M) and windows (0.33M).
- **CPU updates:** about 4 ms per frame; people `update()` is about 3 ms and traffic about 0.7–0.95 ms.
- **Bench noise:** the bench varies by ±7 ms between runs at the same view. Make it trustworthy before measuring optimisations.
